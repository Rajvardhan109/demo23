01 — Background

Study material dies in chat history.

  • Buried within hours — notes sink under new messages in the same thread they were shared in.
  • No structure — nothing is organized by subject, unit, or topic.
  • No quality signal — a rushed photo of a whiteboard sits next to a properly typed summary, indistinguishable.
  • Unsearchable — finding one file means scrolling past hundreds of irrelevant messages.
  • No metadata — downloaded files pile up in a folder with names like IMG_2041.jpg.
Already built

The Smart Notes Sharing System

Our team's existing platform already solves the structural half of this problem: a centralized place to register, upload notes tagged by subject and unit, browse, search by keyword, download, and rate. What it doesn't do yet is think — that's what the three layers on this site add.

03 — Objectives

What this extension is built to achieve

01

Centralized intelligence

Add a recommendation layer on top of the existing platform that actively guides students to relevant material.

02

Personalization at scale

Individualized suggestions for hundreds of concurrent users based on their unique learning profile.

03

Quality amplification

Automatically surface the strongest notes and cut down the manual effort of judging what's worth reading.

04

NLP-powered search

Replace keyword matching with natural-language understanding, in plain English or Hindi.

05

Study path optimization

Guide students through a logical topic progression instead of leaving them to navigate at random.

06

Engagement & retention

Reduce academic stress through proactive, well-timed suggestions rather than passive storage.

07

Scalability

Design the AI layer to grow from a single department to the entire university ecosystem.

Conclusion

The base system fixed storage. This fixes discovery.

Grounded in real student pain points at K.R. Mangalam University and built on a working platform, this AI layer is practical within an academic semester and scalable to the whole institution.
