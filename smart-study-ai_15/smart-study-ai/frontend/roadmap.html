Measurable
  • Improved note discovery rates over time.
  • Rating distribution shifts toward higher-quality notes as engagement grows.
  • Personalized dashboards serving relevant content within 2 seconds of login.
Student impact
  • Finds the right notes instantly, without manual scrolling.
  • Structured, AI-guided progression through subjects.
  • Shared ratings and annotations build collective intelligence.
  • Gap detection prevents last-minute cramming.
  • Top-quality notes reach every student, not just the well-connected ones.
Institutional impact
  • A scalable academic knowledge base that grows smarter over time.
  • Faculty analytics on which topics students struggle with most.
  • Positions K.R. Mangalam University as a leader in AI-enhanced academic infrastructure.
  • Extensible to video lectures, voice notes, and ERP integration.
Scope & future roadmap

Where it starts, where it goes

Current scope

  • College-level deployment across all departments of K.R. Mangalam University.
  • Text-based notes (PDF / image) with AI-powered organization and recommendation.
  • Web-based platform, accessible on desktop and mobile browsers.

Future scope

  • AI-based summarization — auto-generate unit summaries from uploaded notes.
  • Video lecture integration and recommendation.
  • Voice notes and audio transcription support.
  • ERP integration for automated subject-enrollment syncing.
  • Multi-language support — Hindi and English.
  • Mobile app with offline AI inference.
